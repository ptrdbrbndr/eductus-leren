Deel 22 — Examentraining CCBA + portfolio en BA-urenlogboek

Werkboek · Ductus-cursus Business-analyse · niveau 2 · deel 22 van 30

Het CCBA-examen wordt, net als het herziene ECBA-examen, uitsluitend in het Engels aangeboden. Het onderstaande proefexamen is daarom in het Engels. Gebruik de begrippenlijsten uit niveau 1 als basis en vul ze aan met de Engelse termen die in dit niveau zijn gebruikt (traceerbaarheid, prioritering, verificatie/validatie, enzovoort).

Deel A — Proefexamen (36 vragen, representatieve selectie)

Zes vragen per kennisgebied. Het echte examen telt 130 vragen in 180 minuten; deze representatieve selectie is bedoeld om het scenariogebonden karakter te oefenen, niet als volledige voorbereiding.

Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring (6 vragen)

1. A programme has four sub-projects, each choosing its own analysis approach. What should be consistent across all four, according to good BAPM practice? A) The exact same predictive or adaptive approach B) The process by which each approach is chosen and justified C) The seniority of each analyst D) The software tool used — Antwoord: B

2. Monitoring analysis progress should track: A) Only the progress of the technical build B) The progress of the analysis work itself, separate from build progress C) Only stakeholder satisfaction scores D) The number of meetings held — Antwoord: B

3. A shared decision log between business analysis and architecture disciplines primarily helps prevent: A) Budget overruns B) Contradictory assumptions being independently recorded C) Slow requirements elicitation D) Poor user interface design — Antwoord: B

4. Which best reflects good information management for a decision log? A) Anyone may mark any decision as final at any time B) A single, authoritative location with clear ownership for changes C) Each analyst keeps a private, unshared record D) Decisions are recorded only after the project ends — Antwoord: B

5. Assessing the performance of the business analysis function itself, separate from the solution’s performance, is most useful for: A) Replacing the need for solution evaluation B) Identifying whether decisions are being made and recorded with sufficient quality C) Eliminating the need for stakeholder engagement D) Determining project budget — Antwoord: B

6. A default to “how we’ve always approached projects here” without evaluating the current situation is best described as: A) A validated approach B) An approach adopted by habit rather than assessment C) An adaptive approach D) A risk-free choice — Antwoord: B

Elicitation and Collaboration (6 vragen)

7. Which is most important when preparing a session with multiple stakeholders holding conflicting interests? A) Keeping the participant list as small as possible regardless of relevance B) Explicit ground rules established before the discussion begins C) Avoiding any mention of prior disagreements D) Ensuring only senior stakeholders attend — Antwoord: B

8. In a session with several stakeholders, combining the facilitator role with an inhoudelijke stakeholder role is risky primarily because: A) It takes more time B) The facilitator may unintentionally steer the discussion toward their own interest C) It requires more materials D) It reduces the number of attendees — Antwoord: B

9. A stakeholder who does not get the outcome they wanted should, at minimum: A) Be excluded from future sessions B) Have their concern explicitly acknowledged and visibly carried forward C) Be told the decision is final with no explanation D) Not be informed of the outcome — Antwoord: B

10. Which signals a shift from productive tension to destructive conflict? A) Participants disagree about the content of a proposal B) Participants begin attacking each other rather than addressing the topic C) A participant asks a clarifying question D) A session runs slightly over time — Antwoord: B

11. Confirming elicitation output with all participants after a session primarily protects against: A) Excessive documentation B) The loudest voice or the scribe’s notes dominating the shared understanding C) Running out of meeting room bookings D) Legal liability — Antwoord: B

12. A minimum viable outcome, defined before a session begins, is most useful because: A) It guarantees the session’s main goal will be achieved B) It ensures some value is captured even if the larger goal is not met C) It replaces the need for a facilitator D) It shortens the session automatically — Antwoord: B

Requirements Life Cycle Management (6 vragen)

13. Backward traceability for a requirement primarily shows: A) What design and test elements depend on it B) Where it originated — the need, stakeholder, or decision behind it C) Its priority ranking D) Its current approval status only — Antwoord: B

14. Forward traceability for a requirement primarily shows: A) Its origin B) What downstream design, test, and outcome measures depend on it C) Who wrote it D) When it was created — Antwoord: B

15. A requirement with no traceable connection to any downstream artifact most likely indicates: A) A well-scoped, independent requirement B) Either an unnecessary requirement or insufficient traceability C) A high-priority requirement D) A validated requirement — Antwoord: B

16. When assessing a proposed change, the size of the change in wording: A) Reliably predicts its downstream impact B) Does not reliably predict its downstream impact — impact must be traced C) Is the only factor that matters D) Should be ignored entirely — Antwoord: B

17. Which combination of factors best supports a prioritization decision? A) Value alone B) Value, urgency, risk of not doing it, and dependency, considered together C) Whichever stakeholder asks most persistently D) Development cost alone — Antwoord: B

18. Approving a change without recording who decided, on what basis, and which traced requirements were updated is best described as: A) Efficient decision-making B) A repetition of an “approach by habit” failure at a smaller scale C) Best practice for fast-moving teams D) Irrelevant to requirements quality — Antwoord: B

Requirements Analysis and Design Definition (6 vragen)

19. A requirement’s core question is about sequencing and timing. The most appropriate model form is: A) A business rule table B) A process model C) A pure data model D) A user story alone — Antwoord: B

20. A requirement’s core question is about which outcome applies given a combination of conditions. The most appropriate model form is: A) A process model B) A decision table / business rule C) A user story alone D) An entity relationship diagram alone — Antwoord: B

21. Choosing one model form over another always means: A) Nothing is lost B) Something about the requirement’s other aspects is given up C) The requirement becomes invalid D) No further analysis is needed — Antwoord: B

22. Which best distinguishes verification from validation of a requirement? A) They are the same activity B) Verification checks internal quality; validation checks fit with the true stakeholder need C) Verification is done by testers only D) Validation is only relevant in agile approaches — Antwoord: B

23. A requirement stating “processed quickly” fails primarily on which quality characteristic? A) Feasibility B) Testability/clarity — it lacks a measurable threshold C) Consistency D) Prioritization — Antwoord: B

24. When rejecting a requirement from a peer with significant domain expertise, the most effective opening: A) States only what is wrong, without further context B) Acknowledges the overall quality of the work before raising the specific concern C) Avoids raising the concern to preserve the relationship D) Is identical in tone regardless of who owns the requirement — Antwoord: B

Strategy Analysis (6 vragen)

25. A description of the current state that uses judgmental language (“a mess of ad hoc solutions”) rather than verifiable facts is: A) Equally useful as a verifiable description B) Less useful, since it cannot be tested or repeated by others C) The preferred format for strategy analysis D) Required for stakeholder buy-in — Antwoord: B

26. The “gap” between current and future state is best understood as: A) A single, uniform piece of work B) Often multiple sub-gaps: technical, organizational, knowledge, and capacity C) Always primarily technical D) Irrelevant once the future state is defined — Antwoord: B

27. A constraint that the organization cannot change in the short term, such as a legacy system’s capacity limit, should be treated as: A) An assumption to be validated B) A boundary condition to design within, and potentially also a risk C) Irrelevant to strategy analysis D) A stakeholder concern only — Antwoord: B

28. Four stakeholders describe the current situation in four different ways. The best next step is: A) Pick the most senior stakeholder’s version B) Test each description against available evidence before proceeding C) Average the four descriptions D) Ignore all four and start from scratch — Antwoord: B

29. An assumption about the current state that is never verified before a long initiative begins primarily risks: A) Faster delivery B) The initiative proceeding in the wrong direction for its entire duration C) Lower cost D) Improved stakeholder trust — Antwoord: B

30. Risks and constraints should be documented: A) Together, as a single undifferentiated list B) Separately, since they imply different responses C) Only if they are technical D) Only after the initiative is complete — Antwoord: B

Solution Evaluation (6 vragen)

31. A solution performs technically as designed, but the targeted value is not fully realized. The most likely explanation to investigate first, given no evidence of technical defects, is: A) A defect in the solution itself B) A gap in adoption or usage C) The measurement tool is broken D) The original need no longer exists — Antwoord: B

32. Which best distinguishes a solution defect from an adoption gap? A) They are the same and require the same fix B) A defect means the solution doesn’t work as designed; an adoption gap means it works but isn’t used as intended C) A defect is always more expensive to fix D) An adoption gap cannot be measured — Antwoord: B

33. A recommendation after evaluation that only ever confirms the original decision, regardless of new evidence, is at risk of: A) Being unusually rigorous B) Sunk-cost bias — defending a prior decision instead of assessing it fresh C) Being too critical D) Ignoring stakeholder input — Antwoord: B

34. Lowering a threshold in a business rule to force more automation is an appropriate response when the value gap is caused by: A) A lack of user trust in the automation B) A technical miscalibration of that same threshold C) Any type of value gap D) Insufficient documentation — Antwoord: B

35. A stakeholder continues manually double-checking an automated process months after go-live. This is most likely evidence of: A) A successful solution B) An unresolved concern from an earlier stage that was acknowledged but not fully addressed C) A technical defect requiring an immediate fix D) Irrelevant behavior that can be ignored — Antwoord: B

36. Solution evaluation should compare realized value against: A) A vague sense of whether things feel better B) The specific baseline, target, and measure defined before the change C) Only the project budget D) Competitor performance — Antwoord: B

Deel B — De capstone: samenhang over vier deelprojecten

De opdracht

Verdedig in vijftien minuten hoe de elf instrumenten van niveau 2 (aanpakblad, besluitenlogboek, situatieblad, elicitatieplan, workshopdraaiboek, traceerlijn, prioriteringsblad, modelkeuzetabel, requirementskeuring, drie-horizontenkaart, waardemeetblad) samen borgen dat de vier deelprojecten van het Programma Mutatieplatform niet ongecoördineerd naast elkaar zijn gaan werken.

Rollen

  • Iris Tanghe — vraagt naar de samenhang zelf.
  • Bram Wolfslag — vraagt naar de aansluiting met de architectuurlijn.
  • Ruud Kalsbeek — vraagt of Karins ervaring (deel 15, 21) een uitzondering is of een structureel patroon.

Rubric

Samenhang (zwaarste weging) · Eerlijkheid over wat is afgewezen of misgegaan · Standvastigheid onder tegenspraak. Geen puntenschema; mondelinge terugkoppeling na afloop.

Deel C — Het BA-urenlogboek

Opdracht: vul je eigen logboek in (individueel, 45 minuten)

Vul voor jezelf, op basis van je eigen werkervaring (binnen of buiten een cursuscontext), een BA-urenlogboek in voor alle zes kennisgebieden:

Kennisgebied Geschatte uren Type werk Voorbeeld Mogelijke referent
Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring
Elicitation and Collaboration
Requirements Life Cycle Management
Requirements Analysis and Design Definition
Strategy Analysis
Solution Evaluation

Vul tot slot in: welk kennisgebied heeft de minste uren, en welk soort werk zou dat gat het snelst vullen?

Dit onderdeel wordt niet gedeeld of nagekeken — het dient uitsluitend je eigen portfolio-opbouw. De docent vraagt aan het eind alleen of iedereen het gatveld heeft kunnen invullen.

Antwoordsleutel proefexamen

Alle antwoorden zijn B. Dit is met opzet zo geconstrueerd voor deze representatieve oefenset (elke vraag test hetzelfde soort onderscheid: het methodisch juiste antwoord versus drie afleiders). Het echte examen kent een normale spreiding over A, B, C en D — laat dit tijdens de nabespreking expliciet weten, zodat niemand dit patroon verwacht op het echte examen.
