Deel 10 — Examentraining ECBA + capstone

Werkboek · Ductus-cursus Business-analyse · niveau 1 · deel 10 van 30

Dit werkboek bestaat uit twee delen: een proefexamen in de echte vorm (50 vragen, 75 minuten, Engelstalig) en de capstone-opdracht met rubric. Neem het proefexamen onder examenomstandigheden af: gesloten boek, tijdgebonden, geen overleg.

Deel A — Proefexamen (50 vragen, 75 minuten)

Elke vraag heeft één beste antwoord. De verdeling volgt de officiële blauwdruk: domein 1 (10 vragen), domein 2 (7), domein 3 (3), domeinen 4 t/m 9 (5 elk).

Domein 1 — Understanding Business Analysis (10 vragen)

1. A stakeholder says: "We need a new scheduling tool." What should the analyst do first? A) Start comparing scheduling tools B) Ask what problem the current scheduling approach creates C) Estimate the cost of a new tool D) Write a requirement for a scheduling tool

2. Which best describes the purpose of business analysis? A) Producing complete documentation of a solution B) Enabling change by determining what is needed and why C) Managing a project's timeline and budget D) Building and testing software

3. A team delivers exactly what was specified, on time, within budget. Two months later the initiative is cancelled. What does this most likely indicate? A) The solution was poorly built B) The output was delivered but the outcome and value were never realized C) The budget was too low D) The project manager underperformed

4. Business analysis is best described as being performed by: A) Only people with the job title "business analyst" B) Anyone performing the underlying work of enabling change, regardless of title C) Only staff in IT departments D) Only external consultants

5. A stakeholder claims a new process will "improve efficiency." What is missing from this claim? A) A project plan B) A named stakeholder who benefits and a way to observe the improvement C) A technical architecture D) A signed contract

6. Which is the clearest example of confusing a solution with a problem? A) "Employees report delays in approvals" B) "We need a new approval system" C) "Approvals take an average of five days" D) "Three departments are affected by approval delays"

7. What is the primary risk of accepting a stated solution without exploring the underlying need? A) It takes longer to start the project B) Alternatives and the true problem may never be examined C) It increases documentation D) It reduces stakeholder involvement

8. A colleague says business analysis "only happens in software projects." This statement is: A) Correct, since business analysis requires IT systems B) Incorrect, since business analysis applies wherever change is enabled, across sectors and delivery approaches C) Correct only for government organizations D) Correct only for adaptive approaches

9. Which question best helps trace a requested solution back to the underlying need? A) "Why do you want this?" B) "What would be different if this were solved?" C) "How much will this cost?" D) "When do you need this by?"

10. A specification is signed off, but no one can explain what problem it solves. This is most closely associated with: A) A validated need B) A solution presented without an underlying need C) A well-scoped change D) A realized outcome

Domein 2 — Attitude for Effective Business Analysis (7 vragen)

11. An analyst records exactly what a stakeholder says without probing further, even when a statement is ambiguous. This behavior best reflects: A) Strong communication skills B) A failure to distinguish recording from analyzing C) Efficient documentation D) Appropriate deference to stakeholder authority

12. Which underlying competency category includes ethical behavior and trustworthiness? A) Analytical thinking and problem solving B) Behavioral characteristics C) Business knowledge D) Tools and technology

13. An analyst hesitates to challenge a senior stakeholder's assumption, believing "it's not my place to question that." This reflects a gap in: A) Technical knowledge B) Attitude — specifically, a belief about one's own role C) Tools and technology D) Business knowledge

14. Which is the best example of professional curiosity in practice? A) Accepting the first explanation offered B) Asking a follow-up question when a term like "all" is used without definition C) Documenting a meeting verbatim D) Declining to ask questions to avoid appearing uninformed

15. Listening is primarily associated with which underlying competency category? A) Interaction skills B) Communication skills C) Tools and technology D) Business knowledge

16. An analyst discovers an uncomfortable finding but does not report it because no one asked. This is most closely related to a gap in: A) Business knowledge B) Ethical behavior C) Tools and technology D) Analytical thinking

17. Underlying competencies are best understood as: A) Fixed personality traits some people have and others do not B) Trainable capabilities that support effective analysis C) Optional skills relevant only to senior analysts D) Substitutes for domain knowledge

Domein 3 — Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring / Establishing Business Analysis (3 vragen)

18. A team defaults to a lengthy upfront specification phase "because that's how projects are always done here," without evaluating the situation. This best illustrates: A) A deliberately chosen predictive approach B) An approach adopted by habit rather than assessment C) An adaptive approach D) A hybrid approach

19. Which factor most strongly favors an adaptive approach over a predictive one? A) The need is well understood and stable B) The need is uncertain and the context is likely to change C) Correcting course later is very costly D) Stakeholders are only available at the start

20. A requirement states: "Users can submit a correction for a closed period." A related statement adds: "via a three-step form with a confirmation screen." The second statement is best classified as: A) A requirement B) A design element C) A constraint D) An assumption

Domein 4 — Change (5 vragen)

21. The difference between scope and impact is best described as: A) Scope is a deliberate boundary; impact exists regardless of that boundary B) They are interchangeable terms C) Impact is always smaller than scope D) Scope is determined after impact is measured

22. A process outside the formal project boundary is affected by a change, but no one identified this beforehand. This is best described as: A) A deliberately excluded impact B) An overlooked impact C) A realized outcome D) A validated requirement

23. Which is NOT one of the four impact types typically assessed for a change? A) Impact on processes B) Impact on people and roles C) Impact on competitor pricing D) Impact on data

24. A decision to exclude a particular impact from scope, with a documented owner and rationale, is best described as: A) An overlooked impact B) A deliberately excluded impact C) A design decision D) A realized value

25. Which practice most directly helps surface impact that would otherwise be missed? A) Relying solely on the original project charter B) Systematically reviewing processes, data, systems, and people affected by a change C) Waiting until after go-live to assess impact D) Limiting analysis to the solution's technical components

Domein 5 — Needs (5 vragen)

26. Which technique is most appropriate when there is reason to believe stated behavior differs from actual behavior? A) A structured interview B) Document analysis C) Observation D) A closed survey

27. A stakeholder says: "This happens a few times a year, I think." How should this be recorded? A) As a confirmed fact with a specific frequency B) As a direct quote, preserving the stated uncertainty C) Omitted, since it is not precise D) Rounded up to a conservative estimate

28. Validating a need primarily involves: A) Documenting it exactly as stated B) Testing it against a second, independent source before treating it as established C) Presenting it to the largest possible audience D) Converting it immediately into a design

29. An analyst separates "the client called to check a submission" (observed) from "the client doesn't trust the system" (interpreted). This practice demonstrates: A) Poor documentation, since the two should be merged B) Appropriate separation of observation from interpretation C) Unnecessary detail D) A design decision

30. A need has been elicited from only one source and has not yet been cross-checked. The most appropriate status for this need is: A) Fully validated B) Rejected C) Pending confirmation D) Out of scope

Domein 6 — Solutions (5 vragen)

31. Why is "do nothing" typically included as an option when evaluating solutions? A) It is usually the best choice B) It provides a comparison baseline to demonstrate the value of other options C) It eliminates the need for feasibility analysis D) It satisfies a mandatory template requirement

32. Which is NOT one of the four feasibility dimensions commonly assessed for a solution option? A) Technical B) Operational C) Astrological D) Financial

33. A recommendation explains why the chosen option was selected but does not mention any alternatives. This recommendation is: A) Complete, since only the chosen option matters B) Incomplete, since it does not explain why other options were rejected C) Ideal, since it avoids confusing the reader D) Only missing a cost estimate

34. An option scores well on technical feasibility but poorly on operational feasibility. This means: A) The option should automatically be rejected B) Feasibility must be weighed across all relevant dimensions before a recommendation is made C) Technical feasibility always outweighs other dimensions D) The option is not worth documenting

35. Which best supports a defensible recommendation? A) Selecting the first viable option identified B) Comparing multiple options and explaining why the strongest rejected option was not chosen C) Avoiding documentation of rejected options to save time D) Choosing the least expensive option regardless of fit

Domein 7 — Stakeholders (5 vragen)

36. Selecting stakeholders to consult primarily based on how easily they can be reached is an example of: A) A valid prioritization method B) Selecting based on availability rather than relevance C) Best practice for representative sampling D) A requirement, not a stakeholder concern

37. Which best distinguishes "interest" from "influence" for a stakeholder? A) They are the same concept B) Interest reflects what a stakeholder gains or loses; influence reflects their power over the outcome C) Influence is always higher for stakeholders with high interest D) Interest only applies to internal stakeholders

38. A group with high interest but low formal influence is often: A) Irrelevant to the analysis B) At risk of being overlooked despite being highly affected C) Automatically included due to their interest D) The same as a decision-maker

39. Which question best tests the representativeness of a stakeholder group already consulted? A) "Did we speak to enough people?" B) "Which affected group would we never have heard from if we only spoke to these people, and is that a coincidence?" C) "Are these stakeholders senior enough?" D) "Did everyone agree with the proposed solution?"

40. Three stakeholders out of a population of several thousand are consulted, all sharing similar characteristics unrepresentative of the wider group. This is best addressed by: A) Concluding the analysis is complete since stakeholders were consulted B) Explicitly assessing whether the consulted group represents the broader population C) Increasing the number of meetings with the same three stakeholders D) Removing stakeholder analysis from the process entirely

Domein 8 — Value (5 vragen)

41. Which best distinguishes output from outcome? A) Output is what is delivered; outcome is the behavior that changes as a result B) They are interchangeable C) Outcome always precedes output D) Output is only relevant in adaptive approaches

42. A solution is delivered on time and within budget, but the targeted behavior never changes. This indicates: A) Full realization of value B) A gap between output and outcome, and therefore unrealized value C) Successful value realization D) An error in the budget estimate

43. A baseline measurement is most useful when it is: A) Estimated after the change is implemented B) Collected using the same measure, representative of the target group, before the change begins C) Based on the most convenient available data, regardless of source D) Optional if the team is confident about the expected result

44. Which best describes "potential value" versus "realized value"? A) Potential value is an estimate made before change; realized value is confirmed through measurement after change B) They mean the same thing C) Realized value always exceeds potential value D) Potential value can only be financial

45. A team never defines in advance what result would indicate the change has failed. This most directly increases the risk of: A) Early, decisive action when problems emerge B) A slow, unnoticed drift into failure without a clear trigger for action C) Excessive documentation D) Faster decision-making

Domein 9 — Context (5 vragen)

46. A hard limit on available budget for a change is best classified as: A) An assumption B) A constraint C) A standard D) An outcome

47. "All employers have internet access during working hours" is best classified, until verified, as: A) A constraint B) A standard C) An assumption D) A validated requirement

48. A style guide that dictates formatting for all customer correspondence is best classified as: A) A constraint B) A standard C) An assumption D) A requirement

49. Which best describes the risk of treating an unverified assumption as an established fact? A) It has no practical risk B) Decisions may be based on something that turns out to be false C) It always improves decision quality D) It eliminates the need for stakeholder analysis

50. Context is best understood as: A) Irrelevant background information B) The circumstances that influence, and are influenced by, the change C) A synonym for scope D) Only relevant to adaptive approaches

Deel B — De capstone

De opdracht

Bereid een verdediging voor van de complete analyse die je in dit niveau hebt opgebouwd voor de herstart van Meridiaan Pensioen. Gebruik al je ingevulde instrumenten: veranderregel, evenwichtsblad, benaderingsblad, impactkaart, behoefteblad, belanghebbendenkaart, optieblad en uitkomstenblad.

De verdediging duurt twintig minuten: tien minuten toelichting, tien minuten kritische bevraging door drie rollen.

De drie rollen

Hanneke Steenbergen — directeur Uitvoering, opdrachtgever. Vraagt naar de veranderregel, de belanghebbenden, en of de aanpak past bij de organisatie.

Thomas Bergkamp — financieel directeur. Vraagt naar de haalbaarheidsafweging: waarom deze optie, wat kost het, wat is afgevallen en waarom.

Ruud Kalsbeek — manager Klantenservice werkgevers. Vraagt naar de impact op zijn team, de representativiteit van de belanghebbendenkaart, en het afbreekcriterium in het uitkomstenblad.

Voorbereiding

Neem in tweetallen of groepjes van drie de rol van elk MT-lid op je en bereid minstens drie kritische vragen per rol voor, gebaseerd op wat een goed ingevulde set instrumenten zou moeten kunnen weerstaan. Wissel deze vragen uit met het groepje dat verdedigt, zodat de verdediging niet van tevoren bekend is.

Rubric

CriteriumWat wordt beoordeeld
Samenhang (zwaarste weging)Sluiten de negen instrumenten logisch op elkaar aan? Is elke latere keuze herleidbaar tot een eerder vastgestelde behoefte of belanghebbende?
Eerlijkheid over wat is afgewezenWordt evenveel aandacht gegeven aan verworpen opties, aannames en belanghebbenden als aan de gekozen route?
Standvastigheid onder tegenspraakWordt een terechte tegenwerping erkend zonder de hele analyse te laten wankelen? Wordt een onterechte tegenwerping weerlegd met argumenten, niet met autoriteit?

Er is geen puntenschema; de docent geeft na afloop van elke verdediging mondelinge terugkoppeling langs deze drie criteria.
